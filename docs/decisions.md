# Design decisions

Each entry: the decision, the alternatives rejected, and why. Entries D-001 to D-012 were agreed with the project owner on 2026-11-04 and are recorded in `docs/plan.md`.
D-013 to D-015 were signed off at the Phase 0 gate; D-016 was agreed at the same time.

| ID | Decision | Rejected | Rationale |
|---|---|---|---|
| D-001 | MCU **STM32WB55RGV6** (VFQFPN68, LCSC C401505) | STM32WB55CGU6 (UFQFPN48) | The firmware pin map (`targets/platform_implementations/st/`) targets the NUCLEO-WB55RG. UFQFPN48 does not bond PB10–PB15 or PC0–PC6 (DS11929 Rev 11, Table 16), so SPI2, the LPTIM1 encoder pins and the IMU data-ready pin don't exist. Every one of its 27 GPIOs would be used. JLC stock: CG 27, RG 225. |
| D-002 | Two 25 mm 12 V brushed gearmotors (JGA25-370 class) with **ABZ encoders** | Steppers; Hall A/B-only encoders | The firmware drives two brushed motors from the DRV8711 bridges in PWM mode (`motion-actuation.md` Part A, REQ-DRIVE-002). The index (Z) is needed for REQ-ODOM-005. **Superseded by D-027 (MG310, Hall A/B, no index) (2026-11-13).** |
| D-003 | **3S LiPo**, 9.0–12.6 V, XT30, external balance charger | 2S (below the DRV8711's 8 V VM minimum), 4S, on-board 18650 | Matches 12 V motors. No on-board charger or BMS. |
| D-004 | IMU **MPU-6500** (C50278) | MPU9250 (EOL, not stocked at JLC), ICM-42688-P, LSM6DSV16X | It is the MPU9250's accel/gyro die: same register map, WHO_AM_I 0x70 instead of 0x71. The existing driver is reused. |
| D-005 | **SPI NOR flash W25Q16JVSSIQ** (C131025) on SPI1, shared with the DRV8711 | Flash on SPI2 (shared with the IMU), QUADSPI | QUADSPI pins collide with the firmware map (PB8/PB9). Sharing SPI2 would add latency to the 1 kHz IMU burst (REQ-RT-004). The DRV8711 uses SPI1 only for configuration and status. |
| D-006 | PCB is a **single vertical plate**: motors on L-brackets at the bottom edge, battery strapped to the back, antenna at the top edge | Deck + side plates; horizontal deck + mast | One board design. The plate is the pendulum body. |
| D-007 | **Chip-down radio**: MLPF-WB55-01E3 IPD + printed IFA + DNP pi tuning network + DNP U.FL; no certification | Chip antenna; FCC/CE programme | No antenna part cost. VNA tuning in the final assembly. Hobby/open-source use. |
| D-008 | **JLCPCB 4-layer 1.6 mm (JLC04161H-7628)**, 1 oz outer / 0.5 oz inner, top-side PCBA | 2.0 mm (antenna retune), 2-layer | Solid L2 GND under the RF path and the motor hot loops. |
| D-009 | **STDC14** debug connector (SWD + NRST + USART1 VCP); **no USB** | USB-C; Tag-Connect | TIM1_CH4 (bridge B2 input) exists only on PA11 = USB_DM (DS11929 Table 18). The firmware's single-timer bridge design and its hardware break need all four channels on TIM1. |
| D-010 | Toolchain **KiCad 10** (10.0.8, `ppa:kicad/kicad-10.0-releases`) + **ngspice** + Python | KiCad 7/8/9 | Owner's choice (2026-11-04). `kicad-cli sch erc` and `pcb drc --schematic-parity` need KiCad 8+. File formats: schematic 20260306, symbol library 20251024, footprint 20260206. Generated files are normalised with `kicad-cli sch/sym/fp upgrade`. |
| D-011 | Power path: **back-to-back P-MOSFETs** (AO4805) switched by a slide switch, with soft-start | Ideal-diode controller; series Schottky | Gives reverse-polarity protection and on/off, and stays **bidirectional** when on, so regenerated motor energy returns to the battery instead of pumping the bus. |
| D-012 | ABZ encoder inputs pass through **SN74LVC3G17** Schmitt buffers powered from 3V3 | Direct connection to FT pins | 5V0 comes up before 3V3, and FT pins accept ≤ 3.6 V while VDD = 0 (ST AN4899). The buffer's I_off and 5 V-tolerant inputs remove that back-powering path and clean up edges from long motor cables. |
| D-013 | Motor **JGA25-370, 12 V, 6000 rpm winding, 1:9.6 (~620 rpm output)**, with ABZ encoder. Fallback: 1:4.4 if only the 4300 rpm winding is available. | (signed off at the Phase 0 gate, 2026-11-04) | S0 (`simulation/REPORT.md` finding 1): recovers 10° in < 1 s at every corner, ≥ 29° recoverable, 58 % duty at 1.0 m/s on 9.9 V. Ratios of 1:20.4 and above can't reach 1.0 m/s. **Superseded by D-027 (2026-11-13).** |
| D-014 | DRV8711 trip **1.3 A** (Rsense 50 mΩ 1206, ISGAIN 40, TORQUE 242); the motor's continuous rating is enforced by a firmware I²t model, not by the trip. Needs REQ-DRIVE-005 reworded in the firmware repo. | (signed off at the Phase 0 gate, 2026-11-04) | S0 finding 2: a trip at the continuous rating can't recover 10° at 1:20.4 and above, and only about 11° at 1:9.6. **Superseded by D-027 (trip 2.0 A; I²t approach kept) (2026-11-13).** |
| D-015 | Motors and brackets on the PCB **bottom** side; all components and the battery (in a keep-out zone) on the **top** side. | (signed off at the Phase 0 gate, 2026-11-04) | JLC single-side PCBA; static lean about 7° (`docs/mechanical.md`), corrected to ≈ 21° at D-024. **Superseded by D-025 for the battery (on the back); parts stay on the front (2026-11-13).** |
| D-016 | ABZ encoder interface: **JST PH 5-pin (VCC, GND, A, B, Z)**, encoder VCC **5V0** with a 0 Ω option for 3V3, open-collector pull-ups to encoder VCC as **DNP** footprints | Waiting for the exact encoder part | Exact motor and encoder not chosen yet (owner, 2026-11-04). Re-pin when the cable is known; the buffer chain (D-012) is unchanged. **Superseded by D-028 (2026-11-13).** |
| D-017 | HSE crystal **NX2016SA-32MHZ-EXS00A-CS06654** (C5441424) | NX2016SA CS06465 (Phase 0 BOM) | CS06465 is a LoRa-spec part: C_L 10 pF and ±30 ppm over temperature, which fail DS11929 Table 52 (C_L 6–8 pF, 20 ppm total). CS06654 is the reference part in DS11929 Table 5 note 1. Found during the Phase 1 datasheet review. |
| D-018 | Buck **TPS62933F** (forced PWM, C5219272) at 1.2 MHz (RT to GND), 4.7 µH Sunlord SWPA4030S | TPS62933 (PFM at light load) | Fixed switching frequency near the radio; the inductor keeps the ripple ≥ 10 % of 3 A for slope compensation (SLUSEA4 s10.2.2.7). |
| D-019 | Logic UVLO through the buck EN divider: **start 9.11 V, stop 8.47 V** (240 k / 36 k) | No hardware cut-off | Stops the logic draining a 3S LiPo below about 2.8 V/cell under load; the firmware arms only above 9.9 V. |
| D-020 | Schematic is **generated** by `kicad/gen/build.py`, which stays the edit path until the first hand edit in KiCad; from then on the `.kicad_sch` files are the source of truth and the generator is retired | Hand-drawn in the GUI | No GUI in this environment. The generated form is reproducible, diffable and checked in CI (regenerate + no diff, ERC, netlist, library). Phase 2 changes (D-021, D-022) went through the generator because no hand edits existed yet. |
| D-021 | Power-switch gate network: R102 **100 k**, C101 **47 nF**, new C102 **1 µF** gate–source | Phase 1 values (10 k, 220 nF, no gate–source cap); eFuse or ideal-diode controller | S1: the Phase 1 network turned the FET on during hot plug even with the switch off (VBAT 10.5 V, 43–45 A spikes above the AO4805's 40 A pulse rating). The new values keep the FET off on hot plug and limit inrush to 0.54 A, with the same parts and no extra ICs. |
| D-022 | **SS34 Schottky** (C8678) from GND to each of the four phase nodes; firmware keeps **IDRIVE 00** | No clamps; series resistors on AOUTx | S2: the dead-time freewheel held the DRV8711 phase pins at −0.73 V for ~3 µs per period (abs max −0.6 V). The clamps give −0.36 V. IDRIVE 11 rings the switch node to +5.5 V. |
| D-023 | TPS62933F verified by datasheet equations; its dynamics are a bench item | Behavioural buck model with guessed compensation | TI's unencrypted TPS62933P model does not run in ngspice-42, and inventing internal-compensation values would make results unreliable. |
| D-024 | Board outline **85 × 130 mm**, 1 mm corner radii; axle 15 mm above the bottom edge | 130 × 170 mm (Phase 0 assumption) | Owner's choice at the Phase 3 start (2026-11-04). S0 rerun (`simulation/REPORT.md` S0): m 0.392 kg, ℓ 26.3 mm, τ 86 ms; 1:9.6 (D-013) still passes. The motors (≈ 67 mm each) now overhang the plate, so gearbox-face brackets need a ≥ 40 mm base leg or a can mount (`docs/mechanical.md`). The rerun also fixed the Phase 0 pivot: the static lean is ≈ 21°, not 7°. **Superseded by D-026 for the axle position (outline unchanged) (2026-11-13).** |
| D-025 | Battery (3S, 75 × 35 × 22 mm) on the **back** face, centred at z 44–79 mm, held by a printed cradle on 4 × M3 holes. The front over it is **THT-free** (SMD parts only), enforced by a B.Cu rule area. | Battery on the front in a part-free zone (D-015) | Owner, gate A v1 review (2026-11-13). Frees the front for parts. S0: lean 17° instead of 26° with the battery on the front. |
| D-026 | Chassis: **WHEELTEC 102 mm U-bracket** (2 mm steel) bolted to the back in the bottom **25 mm strip**. Axle at the strip centre (12.5 mm above the bottom edge), 18.8 mm behind the plate mid-plane. The strip holds no parts and no battery, and has no B.Cu copper. Holes: 4 × M3 NPTH on 64 × 17 mm and 4 × Ø2.4 NPTH on 77 × 14 mm (bracket drawing). | Motor slots on the plate (D-024 default); axle 15 mm above the bottom edge | Owner supplied the bracket (2026-11-13). The legs stand 8.5 mm proud of each side edge; wheels go outside them (track ≈ 0.15 m). |
| D-027 | Motors **MG310 1:20** (7.4 V rated, Hall A/B encoder) on 3S. The firmware **caps the average motor voltage at 7.4 V**. DRV8711 trip **2.0 A** (ISGAIN 20, TORQUE 186), with the 0.5 A continuous rating enforced by firmware I²t. | JGA25-370 1:9.6 (D-013), trip 1.3 A (D-014), ABZ encoders (D-002); 2S battery with a new driver | Owner's motors (2026-11-13). The DRV8711 needs VM ≥ 8 V, so 3S stays. S0: 1.0–1.5 A trips recover only 12–17°, 2.0 A recovers 22°; a 7.4 V cap cruises 1.0 m/s at 57 % of the cap. Stall current is an ASSUMPTION (the listing says '≤ 2 mA'). |
| D-028 | One **JST PH S6B-PH-K-S (C157920)** per motor on the side edges, MG310 pinout (1 M−, 2 encoder VCC, 3 A, 4 B, 5 GND, 6 M+). Z chain removed; the buffer's third input is grounded, so PC5/PC3 read low. | XH 2-pin + PH 5-pin (D-016) | One cable per motor (owner, PH 2.0). The MG310 has no index channel, so REQ-ODOM-005 can't be met with this motor; reported to the firmware. The 2.0 A trip equals the PH contact rating. |
| D-029 | Floorplan: MCU top centre; 2.4 GHz IFA at the top edge, centred above RF1, with a **full-width keep-out band**; IMU beside the MCU; STDC14 at the middle of the right edge; motor connectors on both side edges. | Gate A v1 anchor draft | Owner, gate A v1 review (2026-11-13). The full-width band mirrors SWRA117D's reference (antenna at the board end, ground below only). |
| D-030 | NFC tag **ST25DV04KC-IE8S3** (C5221752, SO-8, NFC Forum Type 5 / ISO 15693, 4 Kbit) on I2C3 (0x53 user, 0x57 system). GPO → **PA2** (EXTI2). PCB coil in the top-right corner with a DNP tuning cap (`docs/nfc.md`). | NT3H2111 SO-8 (17 in stock), M24LR04E (39 in stock), M24SR04 (Type 4, 2.7 V minimum) | Owner request (2026-11-13). In stock (2500), about $0.89. PA2 and EXTI2 are free in the firmware pin map. |
| D-031 | I2C EEPROM **M24C64-RMN6TP** (C79988, 64 Kbit, SOIC-8) on I2C3 at 0x50 (E2..E0 = 0, WC to GND). | 24C02 (2 Kbit), 24C256 | Owner request (2026-11-13). ST part with 104 k in stock and 4 M write cycles; 8 KB holds calibration and configuration with room to spare. |
